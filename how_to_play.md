# How to Play Murder Mystery
Murder Mystery is a social deduction game, typically played on pre-made maps, where one or a small number of players are chosen as Murderers and everyone else is Innocent. The Murderers need to kill all the innocents, whereas the innocents need to kill all the murderers.

Murder Mystery (MM) is a fairly common gamemode even in Minecraft. Unlike most versions of MM, in this version every player has an iron sword, a bow, and infinite arrows, meaning the mode uses minecraft's basic PVP. The only distinction between Murderers and Innocents is that Murderers are allowed to kill.
The typical variation played is Murder Mystery 2 (MM2), where 2 players are the murderers. When more than 1 player is the murderer, innocents are allowed to kill if they are 100% sure if someone is the murderer.
The other variation is MM1, where only 1 player is the murderer. In this version, innocents may only kill the murderer if they are being attacked, or have seen someone get attacked and have not lost line of sight.

## Neutrals
There are two other alignments as well, falling under the umbrella of Neutrals. 
Neutral-Killers (NKs) are a third alignment, where each member is on their own team and wants to be the last one standing. They must also be eliminated for innocents or murderers to win as well. Neutral-Killers often have very powerful or unique effects, and sometimes have alternate win conditions
Neutral-Independents (NIs) are an alignment that are a step removed from the faceoff between the other alignments. NIs can win with other alignments, but also can get caught in the crossfire. Every neutral independent has its own win condition it must meet before the game ends to win, but cannot end the game themselves.

## Roles and Abilities
Ultimate Murder Mystery (UMM) has a large number of roles to play with across the four alignments. Every player gets a star item called a `Role Action` that lets them use their role's ability. Roles can have abilities, restrictions, alternate win conditions, and perhaps even alternate rules.

## Actually Playing With Your Friends
Murder Mystery is typically played in a voice call. All players may speak while the game is going and may use any information to their advantage. Once a player dies, they may not talk for the rest of the game.

## How to Host
There is a setup guide in the `setup.md` file that you can use to setup UMM and certain components. The Settings item has all sorts of stuff to mess around with, but the game should come ready to play after completing the setup guide.

### Player Counts
By default the pack is setup for MM2, which means there are 2 murderers. It's often played with at least 7 people up to 10, and if there's more people then a Neutral Killer often gets enabled. However, if you have less people, it's recommended to change the alignment settings. This can be done in the `Player Role Settings -> Game Role & Alignment Settings` menu, and lets you set the number of each alignment that will appear in the game.

For more information on settings and what they do, check out the About tab in the in-game settings menu.
