# Ultimate Murder Mystery

This pack was developed and programmed by rawsome1234 from start to finish.
Octoyoshi, SkullMasters, Dooplikyu, and TaxDodger12 were large helpers of this pack's development, planning, and execution.
Octoyoshi in particular greatly helped when it came to existing MM roles and what needs to be in here, as well as feedback.

### Extra Credits
Thank you to Daniye for developing the Hide Nametags pack (https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/no-more-names-hide-nametags-in-your-worlds-addon)
    This is what allows this pack to hide player nametags, although of course that addon is not required.
This pack was heavily inspired by Town of Us (ToU), a mod for Among Us (which can be found at https://github.com/AU-Avengers/TOU-Mira) based on Town of Salem.
    Modifiers and Role Guessing were directly inspired by ToU, as well as many of the roles in this pack.

### AI Disclosure
All of the code in this project was written by hand, unless otherwise specified. All research into the poorly documented
    Scripting API was done by hand using the documentation, autocomplete, and experimentation.

## Content Credits 
Some roles and modifiers are so synonymous with Social Deduction games that their origins are hard to determine, or they could be credited to many sources.
    These roles are marked with an SD.
Some roles are legacy roles from MM. These roles either have been reworked or it has unknown player origins. They are marked with (Legacy). If anyone knows the exact player or origin of those roles, please let me know.
Roles/Modifiers inspired by Town of Us are marked with a ToU. If major tweaks were made, other people may be credited.
